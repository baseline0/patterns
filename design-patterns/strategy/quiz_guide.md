# Quiz: Strategy

> Generated from `content/`.

## 1. What problem does Strategy primarily solve?

A. Sharing mutable state across objects
B. Selecting algorithms at runtime without caller conditionals
C. Persisting objects to storage
D. Coordinating distributed transactions

**Answer: B.** Strategy encapsulates interchangeable behaviors behind a common interface.

## 2. In a microservices pricing service, where does Strategy fit best?

A. As a network retry policy between services
B. As pluggable domain policies inside one service
C. As the service discovery mechanism
D. As the database replication protocol

**Answer: B.** It organizes internal behavior variants; cross-service concerns belong to architecture patterns.
