# Quiz: Composite

> Generated from `content/`.

## 1. Composite is most appropriate for:

A. Flat collections with no nesting
B. Tree-like part-whole structures
C. Request/response retries
D. Two-phase commits

**Answer: B.** It models recursive hierarchies behind a uniform interface.

## 2. A trade-off of Composite is:

A. Clients must always know concrete types
B. Constraining allowed child types can become harder
C. It cannot support recursion
D. It removes polymorphism

**Answer: B.** A uniform component interface can weaken compile-time/type-level constraints.
