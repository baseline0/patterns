Cache-aside trades consistency for latency

Statement

Cache-aside reduces read latency and backend load by serving hot data from a cache, at the cost of staleness, invalidation complexity, and failure modes.

Invariant

A cache entry is treated as an optimization: the source of truth remains authoritative, and correctness depends on an explicit freshness or invalidation policy.

Miss populates cacheOn a miss, the application reads from the source of truth and writes the result into the cache for subsequent reads.lookup
Freshness is bounded by policyBecause updates may bypass or race the cache, entries can become stale until TTL expiry or explicit invalidation; stronger consistency requires tighter coordination and higher write cost.miss-fill
Operational trade-offs surfaceBenefits (lower latency, reduced load) trade against cold-start misses, stampedes on expiry, cache eviction, and extra invalidation logic that increases system complexity.lookup, staleness

Complexity

O(1) average cache lookup on hits; end-to-end read cost becomes source-of-truth cost on misses plus invalidation/coordination overhead on writes.
