Halfway Escape Optimization: A Quantum-Inspired Solution for General Optimization Problems

Abstract

Introduces Halfway Escape Optimization, a quantum-inspired metaheuristic that models tunnelling and entanglement effects to move candidates between search regions. The paper evaluates it on fourteen thirty-dimensional benchmark functions against particle swarm, genetic, artificial fish swarm, grey wolf, and quantum-behaved particle swarm optimizers. It then tries two constrained engineering designs and a rice-classification task, reporting competitive optimizer performance and stronger accuracy on the classification example.

Halfway Escape Optimization

A quantum-inspired metaheuristic whose move rule lets candidates leave a local region before settling.

Tunnelling metaphor

The quantum-inspired escape idea that lets a candidate cross a barrier instead of only climbing downhill.

Benchmark suite

Fourteen general optimization functions evaluated at dimension thirty to compare search behaviour.

Established optimizers

PSO, GA, AFSA, GWO, and QPSO, used as comparison points for the new method.

Constrained design problems

Pressure-vessel and tubular-column design tasks used to test feasibility beyond synthetic functions.
